

Outline

Every year, 10000 - 20000 people die and hundreds of thousands of buildings collapse each year due to Earthquakes.
My name is Vihaan Gandhi and I made the app Seismic!

Seismic, in native IOS, serves as a centralized software and hardware application to protect individuals and buildings
from Earthquakes before an earthquake's destructive wave arrives, acts
to make a building safe, measures whether that building's structural behaviour
changed, and lets anyone pull a real building into a 3D simulator and shake it
with a real earthquake record.
